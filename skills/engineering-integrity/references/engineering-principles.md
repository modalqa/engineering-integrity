# Engineering principles

Prefer observable evidence to assumptions, explicit requirements to agent interpretation, and established project conventions to generic preferences. Recommend the smallest correction that restores the required behavior. Verify completion independently of implementation claims.

Choose verification based on affected paths and risk. A small meaningful test can establish more than many unrelated passing tests. Describe concrete failures and uncertainty separately. Keep correction bounded and unresolved issues visible.

An acceptable trade-off explains the competing constraints and carries evidence of maintained required behavior. It is not a reason to hide a failing mandatory criterion. Human approval for risky actions applies at the action boundary; routine authorized checks should continue.
